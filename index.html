About

Profile

I work as a nanofabrication engineer at Microsoft Quantum. Before that I completed an M.S. in Chemical Engineering at Purdue (2026), an M.Tech at IIT Kharagpur and a B.E. at BITS Pilani.

My research covered microfluidic diagnostics, polymer and colloid rheology, and multiphysics modeling of microreactors. The common thread is linking how something is made to how it performs, which is the core problem in device fabrication too.
